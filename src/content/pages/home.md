Washington's clean energy aspirations have been amazing. We passed landmark
legislation like the Climate Commitment Act and Clean Energy Transformation
Act. Big corporations like Microsoft and Amazon announced ambitious
sustainability plans. The cost of clean technology kept plummeting.

Now those commitments are eroding. Data centers demand huge quantities of
electricity. Corporations are reaching for expensive nuclear and natural gas
to fill the gap, and consultants are claiming the Pacific Northwest has no
choice but to burn more fossil fuels. We reject these detrimental conclusions.

The Washington Clean Energy Coalition finds smart energy management, energy
storage, and competitive renewable resources provide readily available
solutions that cost less, improve grid reliability, and eliminate pollution.
