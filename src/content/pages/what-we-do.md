We are a small, all-volunteer group with an outsized focus: making sure
Washington's clean energy plans and policies benefit people, not just
corporations and billionaires.

## Regulatory engagement

At the Washington Utilities and Transportation Commission, we comment
and submit testimony on rate cases, integrated resource plans, and
gas-expansion proposals. We also engage other planning processes that
shape Washington's energy future — bringing a public-interest voice to
rooms often dominated by industry.

## What is our superpower?

We wrestle with the details of policies, regulations, and bills, and
distill the parts the public needs to know. We write and distribute
letters, public comments, and petitions that anyone can read and
understand. By making complex decisions accessible, we help citizens
and organizations participate in them.

## Working with partners

We work alongside allied organizations so clean-energy advocates speak
with an informed, unified voice.
