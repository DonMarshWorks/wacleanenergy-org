The Washington Clean Energy Coalition is an all-volunteer group of
Washingtonians who care about consumers, the environment, and future
generations.

That's probably exactly how you might expect a non-profit to describe
itself. So who are we, really?

Many of us first met representing organizations like the Sierra Club and
the Northwest Energy Coalition in Puget Sound Energy's advisory groups —
citizen members helping shape and critique the utility's long-range
resource plans. It turns out PSE didn't appreciate the input, and
starting in 2021 the company excluded us from further participation. The
Washington Clean Energy Coalition was born shortly after, so that
public-interest voice wouldn't disappear from PSE's planning entirely.

We still submit written comments to PSE, but PSE routinely ignores them. Consequently, we focus on the venues where decisions can actually
shift: the Washington Utilities and Transportation Commission (which
regulates investor-owned utilities like PSE), the state legislature,
and the governor's office.
