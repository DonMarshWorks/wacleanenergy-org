WCEC asked the WUTC to publish a clear process and timeline to finalize
Puget Sound Energy's Clean Energy Implementation Plan, after the
Commission's Order 10 declined to consolidate the CEIP and rate case
dockets. The letter argues the Clean Energy Transformation Act cannot be
delayed because the work spans two dockets.

- Order 10 in Docket UE-220066 denied PSE's motion to consolidate the
  CEIP and rate case dockets.
- WCEC implored the Commission to require PSE to demonstrate meaningful
  and measurable progress on the CEIP rather than letting the docket
  separation slow it down.
- The letter asks the Commission to publish a process and timeline that
  finalizes CEIP implementation before the end of 2022.
- If WUTC staff resources are a limiting factor, WCEC suggests the
  Commission engage qualified consultants to ensure expeditious
  completion.
- WCEC member organizations at the time included Sierra Club, 350
  Seattle, Climate Action Bainbridge, Northwest Energy Coalition, People
  for Climate Action, Union of Concerned Scientists, and Vashon Climate
  Action Group.
