WCEC asked the Washington Utilities and Transportation Commission to
improve Puget Sound Energy's public participation in its long-term
resource planning. The letter documents how PSE has progressively
narrowed public access since the 2015 IRP and identifies three areas
where the company falls short of meaningful engagement.

- PSE's online-only meeting format replaces in-person and hybrid options
  that other agencies (including Bellevue, where PSE is headquartered)
  routinely use.
- The Resource Planning Advisory Group is limited to ~a dozen
  pre-approved professional representatives, categorically excluding
  longtime volunteer participants.
- PSE's use of IAP2 (International Association for Public Participation)
  standards has weakened: 13 of 28 meeting topics in 2024 received no
  IAP2 rating at all.
- Public attendance at both RPAG meetings and webinars has declined
  throughout 2024.
- WCEC asks the UTC to require hybrid meetings, neutral advisory-group
  selection, and consistent IAP2 use.
