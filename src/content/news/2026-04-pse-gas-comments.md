The Washington Clean Energy Coalition has submitted formal comments to the
Washington Utilities and Transportation Commission on Puget Sound Energy's
most recent resource proposal.

## What we asked for

Our comments make three points:

1. New natural gas capacity is incompatible with the state's 2045
   carbon-free electricity mandate.
2. Clean alternatives — wind, solar, storage, and efficiency — can meet
   the same reliability needs.
3. Ratepayers should not be left holding the cost of stranded fossil-fuel
   assets.

## What happens next

The Commission will review comments from all stakeholders before issuing
its decision. We will continue to track the docket and report back here.
