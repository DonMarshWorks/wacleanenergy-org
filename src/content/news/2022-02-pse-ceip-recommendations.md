WCEC distributed a public-facing summary of its recommendations for Puget
Sound Energy's Clean Energy Implementation Plan ahead of the March 2,
2022 WUTC public comment deadline. The two-page document called on the
public to submit comments to UTC and outlined two primary changes.

- PSE should reduce demand more aggressively through Demand Response and
  Time Varying Rates programs.
- PSE should accelerate acquisition of wind, solar, storage, and
  distributed energy resources to reduce reliance on natural gas.
- PSE's forecasting models do not account for climate change, relying on
  90 years of historical temperature data that no longer reflects current
  conditions.
- Summer peak demand is likely to rise as warming continues; PSE should
  accelerate local solar and battery capacity to improve resilience
  during heat dome events.
- The handout encouraged ratepayers and organizations to file written
  comments at utc.wa.gov by 5 p.m. Wednesday, March 2, 2022 (Docket
  UE-210795).
