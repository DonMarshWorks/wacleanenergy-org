In a joint letter to Governor Ferguson, WCEC and Third Act Washington
argued that the Data Center Workgroup's December 1 preliminary
recommendations reflect industry priorities more than the concerns of
voters, ratepayers, and residents. The letter highlights revelations
from the Department of Ecology's minority report that multiple data
center developers have proposed using fossil fuels as their main power
source.

- The Workgroup rejected (by one vote) a recommendation that would have
  required data centers to use clean energy to qualify for tax breaks.
- Ecology's minority report reveals "multiple data center developers
  have shared proposals with Ecology to use fossil fuels as the main
  source of their power."
- The Data Center Coalition stated data centers will use backup
  generators rather than reduce load during energy emergencies —
  degrading air quality in already-overburdened communities.
- The letter makes seven specific policy recommendations spanning clean
  energy, prices, grid stability, water, neighbor quality of life, tax
  equity, and AI's broader future.
- The Yakama Nation's detailed analysis is highlighted as the most
  thoughtful contribution to the Workgroup's deliberations.
