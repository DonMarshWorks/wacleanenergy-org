WCEC challenged Puget Sound Energy's unexplained 50% derate of estimated
peak reduction from its Time Varying Rate (TVR) programs in the Draft
Clean Energy Implementation Plan. The filing notes that PSE's own
consultant — the Brattle Group — never recommended derating TVR benefits,
and asks the UTC to require a clear explanation before allowing the
derate to delay TVR adoption.

- PSE's Draft CEIP Table 4-2 cuts estimated peak reduction from each TVR
  pilot in half, justifying it only via a single footnote stating PSE's
  system is "winter-peaking."
- The Brattle Group, PSE's own TVR consultant, did not propose derating
  in any of the presentations WCEC attended.
- Slower TVR adoption would delay or eliminate the need for a new peaker
  plant PSE wants to begin building shortly — a financial incentive PSE
  has to slow-walk TVR deployment.
- TVRs have been a six-year focus for WCEC members, partly stemming from
  the Energize Eastside debate where coalitions argued ambitious Demand
  Response could eliminate the need for the costly transmission upgrade.
- The filing asks the Commission to demand a clear justification for the
  derating and, absent that, require faster TVR adoption.
