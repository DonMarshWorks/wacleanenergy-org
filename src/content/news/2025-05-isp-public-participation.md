A joint filing by four organizations urged the WUTC to restore
meaningful public participation in PSE's long-term resource planning.
The letter details how PSE's Resource Planning Advisory Group has become
progressively more closed since 2015 and proposes eight specific
improvements.

- PSE's 2023 shift to a Resource Planning Advisory Group with strict
  professional qualifications excluded longtime volunteer participants
  and reduced public input to short comments at meeting close.
- Online-only meetings hide attendance, restrict chat access to RPAG
  members only, and provide no way for the public to contact RPAG
  members.
- The Feedback Report process gives the public 5 working days to comment
  while PSE has 15 to respond, and PSE's responses are often terse or
  dismissive.
- PSE has stopped tagging meeting topics with the IAP2 participation
  level it once committed to.
- The coalition requests hybrid meetings, neutral selection of advisory
  members, transparent communications, equal comment windows, and
  meaningful RPAG agenda input.
